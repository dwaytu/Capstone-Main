# Figures 8-11 Activity Diagram Workflows

## Figure 8. Guard Activity Diagram
Login and validate access, review mission information, then either use non-duty functions or complete readiness, check-in, active duty, incident/support/SOS escalation, check-out, and logout.

## Figure 9. Supervisor Activity Diagram
Login, review workforce status, resolve staffing gaps through replacement coordination, monitor operations and permitted resources, review outcomes, act on issues, and continue or logout.

## Figure 10. Administrator Activity Diagram
Login, review pending work, select a permitted management area, review data or operational status, correct issues, verify updates, and continue or logout.

## Figure 11. Superadmin Activity Diagram
Login, review global status and traceable audit information, address authorized governance or account issues, verify the resulting state, and continue oversight or logout.
